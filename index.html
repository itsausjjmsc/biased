Insight #1: The Paradox of Existence

"If a website is made from a Reddit post about a website, does the website exist before it exists?"

Over-Explanation: This is what philosophers call a "chicken and egg" problem, except it's more like "chicken and slightly different chicken" problem. The website obviously exists because you're looking at it, but philosophically speaking, did it exist in potentia before its creation? We consulted three philosophers, two quantum physicists, and a guy named Derek. Derek said "probably." We're going with Derek's answer.

Insight #2: Meta-Recursion

"This parody is a parody of a parody of reality, which itself might be a simulation."

Over-Explanation: Let's break this down: Reality → Reddit Post → Original Website → This Parody → Your Understanding of This Parody → The Memory You'll Have of This → The Story You'll Tell About This → Someone Else's Interpretation of Your Story. By our calculations, we're approximately 7 layers deep into the irony onion. Crying yet?

Insight #3: The Bias Principle

"Everything is biased, including this statement about bias, and especially your interpretation of it."

Over-Explanation: The name of this website is "Biased" which is ironic because by calling ourselves biased, we're admitting our bias, which makes us honest, but that honesty is itself biased toward appearing honest. It's biases all the way down. We're basically the Inception of cognitive biases. *BWAAAAAM sound effect*

Insight #4: Digital Archaeology

"Future historians will find this and wonder what was wrong with us."

Over-Explanation: Imagine it's the year 3024. A team of digital archaeologists uncovers this website in the Internet Archive. They'll write entire dissertations trying to understand our culture. "Why did they make this?" they'll ask. "What did it mean?" The answer is: nothing and everything. Mostly nothing. But don't tell the future PhD students that; they need the thesis topic.

Insight #5: The Contributor's Dilemma

"Clone it, they said. Contribute, they said. But to what end?"

Over-Explanation: This website is open source, which means you can contribute to the chaos. But here's the thing: every contribution makes it more ridiculous, which is the point, which means succeeding is failing, and failing is succeeding. It's like a koan, but for GitHub. Commit zen, if you will.

Insight #6: The Satire Singularity

"At what point does satire become indistinguishable from what it satirizes?"

Over-Explanation: Poe's Law states that without a clear indicator of intent, parodies of extremism are indistinguishable from the real thing. We've extended this to create Biased's Law: "Any sufficiently over-explained satire becomes unintentionally serious, and any sufficiently serious website becomes unintentionally satirical." We're not sure which side we're on anymore.

❓ FAQ (Frequently Avoided Questions)
